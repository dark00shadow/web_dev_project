About Us

At Equinox, we believe your wardrobe shouldn't be defined by a single setting. Inspired by the natural transitions of our planet, we design premium apparel that adapts gracefully to every shift in the atmosphere.

From the light, breathable linen textures of sun-drenched afternoons to the structured, insulated layers needed to brave a sudden winter frost, Equinox crafts pieces that balance absolute comfort with sharp, modern tailoring. Wherever the seasons take you, we have the layers to match.
